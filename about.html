About

Fifteen years in the field, and still learning.

Biography

Ramesh Baral is a development practitioner and independent consultant working across disability-inclusive development, inclusive education, humanitarian response, and community-based rehabilitation in Nepal and South Asia. Eight years with Humanity & Inclusion built deep expertise in programme management, GEDSI mainstreaming, policy advocacy, capacity building, and safeguarding at local, provincial, and federal levels.

As a certified master trainer in Universal Design for Learning, over 1,000 teachers, educators, and project teams have been trained across all seven provinces and 65+ districts of Nepal. Work has spanned physical rehabilitation, livelihoods, disaster risk reduction, and health system strengthening — including a UNDP-supported earthquake recovery programme.

Academically grounded with an MPhil in Early Childhood Development & Development Studies and a Bachelor's in Legal Studies, with a lasting commitment to OPD empowerment, inclusive policy advocacy, and community-driven development.
